# PLATEAU AWARD 2026 · CITY STRIKE

Original artwork inspired by https://www.mlit.go.jp/plateau-next/award/ ; not an official event identity.

Building: 新宿センタービル / bldg_894e93d9-1141-4534-b03c-1953ef7696ac. Adopted native geometry, UVs and placement are retained. This is a procedural creative paint overlay, not a photograph-derived facade reconstruction.

Version 2 uses angular full-building ribbons in acid yellow, hot pink and cobalt against charcoal, large italic lettering with dark outlines, controlled overspray and plinth halftones. No characters, petals or cute motifs. Four directional typographic hits sit inside a continuous world-space color field. The artwork is baked into an ordinary editable paint layer and supports fading and overpainting. Version 1 remains in existing local gallery history; the new revision has a distinct ID.
